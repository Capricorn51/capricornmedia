The Model

Three things — One partnership

There is a simpler way to bring a digital product to market. You do not need to build it yourself or manage every moving part. You need the right process — and a team that executes it.

01

We Build

We handle everything from concept to final delivery — online courses, ebooks, membership programmes, and template packs. You set the direction. We do the work.

02

You Launch

You bring the audience, the trust, and the platform. You take the product to your followers under your name, on your terms. Revenue is shared — you earn from every launch, and so does Capricorn Media. Your brand carries the product. This partnership amplifies what you have already built.

03

Nobody Knows

We operate entirely behind the scenes. No watermarks. No credits. No trails. Capricorn Media remains invisible to your audience, your customers, and your competitors. What happens in the arrangement stays in the arrangement. Discretion is the product.

The Process

From introduction
to in market

We move efficiently. Every engagement follows the same four-stage process — designed to protect your time, honour your brand, and get the product live without friction.

1

Partner

Submit your application. We review your platform, audience size, niche, and product vision to determine whether there is a strong fit. We are selective — every partnership needs to work for both sides.

2

Discover

We research your audience's pain points, buying behaviour, and the product opportunity available. A product brief is prepared and agreed before anything is built.

3

Create

We build the product end to end — structure, content, design, and delivery assets. You review at key milestones and approve the final product before it goes to market.

4

Launch

You take the product to market under your brand through your existing channels. We provide launch support and remain behind the scenes.

Selectivity

We don't work with everyone

We work with operators who already have audiences, reach, and the credibility to place a product in front of people who trust them. If you have built a platform, a following, or a reputation in your space, you already have the most valuable asset in the equation. We provide the rest.

Our Story

Founded on an Observation

Capricorn Media was founded by Marianne Reiner on a simple observation: creators with audiences rarely have the time, skill, or infrastructure to build something worthy of the trust those audiences have placed in them.

The gap between a creator with genuine reach and a product worthy of their audience is larger than it should be. Capricorn Media exists to close that gap — quietly, professionally, and on terms that work for both sides.

Every partnership is taken seriously. Every product is built with care. And Capricorn Media's name never appears anywhere your audience can see it. That is not a limitation. That is the entire point.
